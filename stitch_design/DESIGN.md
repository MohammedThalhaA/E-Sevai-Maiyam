## Brand & Style
The design system bridges the authority and trust of official government services (Citizen Service Center / E-Sevai) with the frictionless, welcoming, and high-conversion aesthetic of modern consumer SaaS. By moving away from congested, bureaucratic portal layouts, the interface creates an emotional response of clarity, relief, promptness, and utmost institutional reliability for residents of Villivakkam, Chennai.

The visual direction follows a **Modern Minimalist SaaS** style enriched with **subtle atmospheric depth**:
- **Clarity & Spatial Generosity:** Expansive breathing room, tight headline tracking paired with airy body leading, and zero visual clutter.
- **Civic Trust with Contemporary Polish:** Crisp surfaces, hyper-refined pill controls, and subtle pastel-sky gradients that frame public service delivery as effortless and digitally native.
- **Human-Centric Directness:** Micro-interactions, live status signifiers ("Open Now", "Token Available"), and structured categorization (Aadhaar, Passport, Patta, Certificates) styled like modular API integrations.

## Colors
The palette balances warm civil energy with stable municipal trust:

- **Primary Accent (`#F5A623` - Saffron Amber):** Drives key actions, primary CTA pills, high-priority status notifications, and eyebrow focus tags. Conveys warmth, civic pride, and immediate utility without the harshness of generic warning ambers.
- **Supporting Accent (`#1E8E5A` - Emerald Green):** Represents official authorization, successful application states, live office operating indicators ("Open Today"), and verified credentials.
- **Canvas Gradient (`#EAF2FB` to `#FFFFFF`):** A gentle, pale sky-blue gradient wash at the top of viewport sections fading smoothly into clean architectural white, evoking transparency and digital modernity.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container planes sitting above the tinted atmosphere.
- **Typography & Neutrals:**
  - `Text Primary` (`#141B24`): High-contrast, near-black ink tone ensuring accessible legibility across dense civic documentation tables.
  - `Text Secondary` (`#6B7280`): Refined slate gray for instructional copy, metadata, and micro-labels.
  - `Border Subtle` (`#E2E8F0`): Fine structural boundary lines for cards, pill outlines, and table divisions.

## Typography
This system leverages a single high-performance geometric grotesque (`Inter`) unified across display, reading, and UI utility roles:

- **Tight Hero & Title Rhythm:** Display and headline levels feature tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700-800) to deliver the bold, confident punch of leading tech landing pages.
- **Generous Body Metrics:** Standard reading text employs open line spacing (`1.6x` body ratio) to allow users to digest complex procedural guidelines, required documents checklists, and pricing with zero cognitive strain.
- **Case Styling:** Maintain title case for headings and sentence case for body and supporting descriptions. Badges and eyebrow tags use uppercase or semi-bold sentence case with positive letter spacing (`0.02em`) for immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a clean, centralized 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px` on desktop displays.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** 12 columns, `32px` gutter, `48px` page margins. Centered container layout.
  - **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`):** 8 columns, `24px` gutter, `32px` page margins.
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4 columns, `16px` gutter, `20px` margins. Layout collapses to vertical stacking.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Base rhythm scales exponentially (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 80px, 120px). Hero zones and service categories maintain generous negative space to cultivate an open, welcoming environment.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through a combination of crisp pure-white surfaces, fine low-contrast borders, and ambient multi-layered shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Atmospheric soft pastel gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, #EAF2FB 0%, #FFFFFF 35%, #FFFFFF 100%)`).
- **Level 1 (Interactive Cards & Service Tiles):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), single-pixel perimeter line `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and ambient feather shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 27, 36, 0.03), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(20, 27, 36, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover State / Floating Badges / Sticky Header):** Elevates slightly on pointer interaction:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -3px rgba(20, 27, 36, 0.06), 0 20px 35px -8px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.12)`. Subtle saffron bloom highlights active civic cards.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Token Dispensers / Overlays):**
  `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(20, 27, 36, 0.18)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy harmonizes broad, welcoming containment cards with ultra-soft, fully-pill interactive elements:

- **Interactive Elements (Pill-Shaped):** Buttons, search bars, filter tags, active status capsules, and input chips use `border-radius: 9999px` (fully rounded-full).
- **Cards & Functional Modules:** Containers, feature cards, and credential displays use soft geometric corners between `16px` (`rounded-xl`) and `24px` (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Floating SaaS Asset Clusters:** Sub-elements, icons, and micro-illustrations are contained in soft rounded-rectangles (`12px` to `16px`) to anchor visual interest in hero compositions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Button:** Solid fill `#F5A623`, pure white `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full` (`9999px`), `12px 28px` padding. Transitions on hover with a micro-scale (`scale-102`) and ambient amber shadow (`rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Pill Button:** Outline style with `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, background transparent or `#FFFFFF`, text `#141B24`. On hover, background shifts to `#EAF2FB` with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Pill Button:** No border, text `#1E8E5A` or `#F5A623`, subtle background fill on hover (`rgba(30, 142, 90, 0.08)`).

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Verification / Authorization Badge:** Pill or `12px` rounded square. Background `rgba(30, 142, 90, 0.10)`, text `#1E8E5A`, leading Lucide check or shield icon (`14px`).
- **Live Indicator ("Open Now"):** Pill container with a pulsing emerald dot (`#1E8E5A`) and text "Open Now • Villivakkam Branch".
- **Eyebrow Category Badge:** Background `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.12)`, text `#B87407`, semi-bold uppercase, `rounded-full`, padding `4px 14px`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Service Cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Patta, Income Certificate):** `#FFFFFF` surface, `20px` corner radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Includes a floating icon well (`48px x 48px`, `12px` rounded corner, tinted pastel background), title, document requirements count, and an arrow indicator.
- **Elevation on Hover:** Translates `-4px` vertically with a gentle amber/slate glow.

### Form Inputs & Service Search Bar
- **Search Pill Input:** Centered hero search element. Full pill radius (`9999px`), `#FFFFFF` fill, `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, height `56px`, leading search icon, trailing "Search Services" primary pill button embedded within the input frame.
- **Standard Input Fields:** Height `48px`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`, border `#E2E8F0`, focus state adds `2px ring` in `#F5A623` with no offset.

### Floating Illustration Clusters
- **Hero Cluster:** Floating mini-cards displaying live stats ("100% Authorized", "Avg. Time: 12 Mins", "Govt of Tamil Nadu & Central Services") arranged around the central value proposition with soft drop shadows and subtle CSS floating animations.
